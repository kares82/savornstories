I bring you up to the clifftop Château-Musée de Dieppe for sweeping views over the Channel. It's a good place to start, since you can see most of the town and coastline from up there before we go back down into the story.

Then we follow the story of the 1942 Dieppe Raid along the very seafront where it happened. It's a moving chapter of the Second World War that most visitors never hear about, and walking the actual ground makes it land differently than reading about it.
