I walk with you along the white chalk cliffs that once inspired the Impressionist painters, from Dieppe's seafront to the quiet village of Pourville-sur-Mer.

The sea air is fresh, the Channel views are wide the whole way, and the pace is easy enough for pretty much anyone. It's the kind of walk where the scenery does most of the talking, and I fill in the rest.
