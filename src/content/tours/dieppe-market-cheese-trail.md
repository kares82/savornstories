In Dieppe, I take you through the Saturday market, one of the oldest and liveliest in France, past stalls of Norman cheese, cider and fresh produce. I like to slow down here rather than rush through, since the market itself is half the experience.

Afterward, we sit down together for a guided tasting of Camembert, Neufchâtel, Pont-l'Évêque and Livarot, paired with a local cider. I explain what makes each cheese Norman rather than just French, and why this region ended up producing all four.
