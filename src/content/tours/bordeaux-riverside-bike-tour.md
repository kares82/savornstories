In Bordeaux, I lead a bike tour along the Garonne river, riding the flat path that runs right through the city. Guests meet me near the river, and we ride together past the eighteenth century waterfront, through Chartrons where the wine merchants once kept their warehouses, and out toward the old submarine base that the city has since turned into something completely different.

The path is flat the whole way, so anyone who can ride a bike comfortably can do this tour, no experience needed. We stop several times along the route, including a coffee stop, so the ride never feels rushed.

It usually takes about three hours to cover roughly twelve kilometers, and it gives visitors who only have one day in Bordeaux a real sense of the whole city rather than just one neighborhood. Bikes and helmets are provided.
